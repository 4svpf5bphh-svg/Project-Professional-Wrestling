# PPW autonomous creative contract parity — development wave (11 October 2026)

**CI-only WIP.** Built after PR #20. No merge, Vercel preview, production deployment, or World reset.

## Completed in this wave
- Reuses one deterministic, fictional creative agreement score for human booking proposals and autonomous AI/staff matches. No human-online activity bonus and no inferred real-life wrestler personalities.
- Uses the strongest applicable signed creative-rights clause when contracts overlap. A weaker appearance contract cannot bypass a stronger creative promise.
- For *automatically generated* matches, a protected-finishes clause causes the AI/staff booker to write a non-clean, non-submission finish **before** the card resolves. Winner-side authorship remains with the generated booking algorithm. The protected change is explicitly recorded in World history.
- FULL_APPROVAL wrestlers accept/decline automated match work using the same decision threshold; refusing wrestlers are removed from the automated card pool, and approved actual match assignments are recorded.
- Already-authored human matches are never auto-rewritten. Existing player-specific consent records and the card/commitment guards continue to apply.
- Dedicated automated AI and Routine Continuity regressions are added to full CI.

## Still required before enabling paid creative-control clauses
- **No player-facing creative levels yet.**
- This is a first complete autonomous booking rule, not a detailed AI negotiator. FULL_APPROVAL currently decides whether to accept an automatically assembled match assignment from fictional World-state willingness, not opponent-specific dialogue or advance programme agreements.
- If too many full-approval wrestlers withhold consent, a previously scheduled show may be unable to reach minimum participants and can be cancelled. Review substitution and notice policy, but never breach wrestler rights or manufacture dates.
- Staff takeovers following a blocked human card should be audited in multi-promotion test Worlds so no prior unapproved scripted result is later replayed.
- The pending-card-withdraw action is the next separately reviewed wave, followed by standing programme agreements and financial balancing.
